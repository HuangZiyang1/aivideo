# Segment B — 15s — Flower viewing macro (Visuals-only)

Objective: Macro inspection of blossoms and gentle interaction; no picking.

Global settings
- Style: vintage semi-realistic; meadow blossoms; matte textures; natural patina.
- Environment: wildflowers with visible pollen grains (50–100 µm); basket nearby soft-focus; avoid modern jewelry.
- Camera: macro/close alternation; manual focus 20–40 cm; exposure locked; hard cuts; no rack focus; shallow DOF for texture.
- Physics: right-side light; wind right→left (0.3–0.4 m/s); petal lift angle 8–14°; blade tip micro-oscillation 0.9–1.3 s period; shadows coherent.
- Safety: minimal hand motion; do not harm insects; hands clean and natural.

Timeline (0.0–15.0s)
0.0–3.0s (macro petal): Petal velvet texture; pollen grains (50–100 µm) lightly dust anther; slight lift.
3.0–7.8s (close stamen): Stamen filaments detail; pollen largely static; breeze nudges petal within 8–14°.
7.8–12.0s (close finger): Finger gently lifts petal; never pluck; maintain focus ~25–30 cm; ladybug may pass slowly (~0.01 m/s) unharmed.
12.0–15.0s (macro hold): Hold blossom detail; exposure stable; clean end; no wing blur warps.
