# Segment B — 15s — Flower viewing macro (Visuals-only)

Objective: Macro inspection of blossoms and gentle interaction; no picking.

Global settings
- Style: vintage semi-realistic; meadow blossoms.
- Environment: wildflowers; basket nearby; avoid modern jewelry.
- Camera: macro/close alternation; manual focus 20–40 cm; exposure locked; hard cuts; no rack focus.
- Physics: right-side light; wind right→left (0.3–0.4 m/s); petal lift angle 8–14°; shadows coherent.
- Safety: minimal hand motion; do not harm insects.

Timeline (0.0–15.0s)
0.0–3.0s (macro petal): Petal surface texture; pollen grains visible; slight lift.
3.0–7.8s (close stamen): Stamen detail; pollen static; breeze nudges petal.
7.8–12.0s (close finger): Finger gently lifts petal; no pluck; maintain focus.
12.0–15.0s (macro hold): Hold blossom detail; exposure stable; clean end.
