# Sora Script (English) — Chunfen · Yaochun / Bite-Spring Foods — ~54s, visuals-only

Objective (final policy): Master reference for a ~54-second visuals-only sequence (silent, no on-screen text). Generation will be executed as four clips (15s + 15s + 12s + 12s). No entrance/exit effects; transitions are direct cuts. Style: vintage aesthetic; physics: realistic light/wind/shadows. Mirrors split segments for review and includes their constraints.

Global settings
- Aspect ratio: 16:9; Total: ~54 seconds.
- Style: vintage, semi-realistic; muted earthy palette; gentle dawn haze.
- Environment: early-spring market, simple home kitchen, courtyard table; period-correct utensils; avoid modern packaging/plastics/glossy paint.
- Camera: tripod-stable; exposure locked; fixed focus per shot; macro focus ranges 20–60 cm; no zoom/rack focus/auto-reframe; hard cuts only; stable white balance.
- Light & wind: morning sidelight from right; wind right→left (0.2–0.4 m/s); shadows coherent left; steam/smoke respond physically.
- Safety: no entrance/exit effects; no focus hunting; no exposure pulsing; avoid stabilization warps.
- Text & VO: IGNORE during generation (visuals-only). Timing for post.

Timeline & directions (0.0–54.0s)
0.0–15.0s (Segment A — Market: spring chives, toon shoots, bamboo shoots, radish, spring roll wrappers)
- Visuals: vendor stalls with bunches of spring chives, small purple toon (Chinese cedar) shoots, bamboo shoots, white radishes; a stack of spring roll wrappers; weighing on balance scale; paper wraps rustle.
- Camera: chest-height medium-wide; hard cuts to macro/close; exposure locked; manual focus (macro 30–60 cm); no zoom/rack focus.
- Physics: wind right→left (0.2–0.4 m/s) lifts wrapper edges 12–18° (oscillation ~1.2 s); chive tips sway A≈0.2–0.3 m/s; shadows fall left.

- Visuals: rinse chives; peel bamboo shoots; slice radish thin; quick blanch toon buds; stack spring roll wrappers.

- Physics: droplet diameter 2–4 mm; fall with wind drift (right→left); blade entry clean; blanch steam low; shadows coherent.

- Physics: steam plume rise rate ~0.6 m/s then diffuse; bubble diameter ~1–3 mm; wind right→left shifts steam tail; shadows coherent.
- Details: bamboo spatula; cast-iron/wok patina; avoid modern gas hob visuals.

42.0–54.0s (Segment D — Serving & first bite)
- Visuals: plating on simple clay/wood ware; communal table; first bite of spring roll/chive dish; crumbs fall; gentle steam.
- Camera: mid→close; exposure/focus locked; hold on bite moment; hard cut to finished plate; stable white balance.
- Physics: crumb fall ballistic with slight left drift; crumb size 2–6 mm; steam wisps ~0.4–0.6 m/s; shadows consistent.
- Ending: clean hold on plated dish; no exit effect.
