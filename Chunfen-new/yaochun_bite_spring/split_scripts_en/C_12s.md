# Segment C — 12s — Cooking (Visuals-only)

Objective: Capture oil shimmer onset, controlled toss, and steam behavior with physically consistent cues.

Global settings
- Style: vintage semi-realistic; wok patina; bamboo spatula; cast-iron texture.
- Environment: simple hearth; avoid modern stovetop details; no digital displays.
- Camera: medium with macro inserts; exposure locked; macro focus ~20–30 cm; no rack focus; hard cuts.
- Physics: right-side light; wind right→left (0.2–0.4 m/s); oil shimmer specular ~0.3→0.4; bubble diameter 1–3 mm; steam rise ~0.6 m/s then diffuse.
- Safety: shadows consistent; avoid motion warps; no steam over-enhancement.

Timeline (0.0–12.0s)
0.0–2.8s (macro shimmer): Oil shimmer onset; micro ripples; bubble frequency ramps; specular highlights increase to ~0.4.
2.8–7.2s (medium toss): Quick toss of chives egg / toon scramble; spatula trajectory smooth; steam plume tail leans left; maintain pan stability.
7.2–12.0s (close stir/roll): Radish stir-fry; begin rolling spring rolls pre-fry; confirm exposure/focus lock; clean end hold.
