# Segment B — 15s — Prep (Visuals-only)

Objective: Execute washing, peeling, slicing, and quick blanching with tactile micro-details.

Global settings
- Style: vintage semi-realistic; simple kitchen; warm dawn tone.
- Environment: matte wood board (knife marks); cloth towel (coarse weave); earthenware bowl; avoid stainless sheen/plastic.
- Camera: intercut macro/close; exposure locked; manual focus ~30–40 cm macro, ~60–80 cm close; hard cuts; no rack focus.
- Physics: right-side light; wind right→left (0.2–0.4 m/s); droplets drift; blade entry clean; low steam density; shadows coherent.
- Safety: no micro flicker; avoid over-sharpen halos; keep white balance stable.

Timeline (0.0–15.0s)
0.0–3.2s (macro rinse): Rinse chives; droplet diameter 2–4 mm; fall path slight left drift; surface tension beads visible.
3.2–6.8s (close peel bamboo): Peel bamboo shoot; show fiber lamination; audible implied snap (silent); fracture line clean; avoid gloss.
6.8–10.6s (macro slice radish): Slice thickness ~1–2 mm; translucency gradient; board knife marks; blade speed consistent; no motion warp.
10.6–15.0s (close blanch toon): Quick blanch (<3 s visual); low steam plume; towel pat-dry—cloth fiber compress/rebound; end clean.
