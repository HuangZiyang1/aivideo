# Sora Script (English) — Chunfen · Egg Balancing — ~48s, visuals-only

Objective (final policy): Master reference for a ~48-second visuals-only sequence (silent, no on-screen text). Generation will be executed as four clips (12s + 12s + 12s + 12s). No entrance/exit effects; transitions are direct cuts. Style: vintage aesthetic; physics: realistic light/shadow/wind. For this custom, light shifts to near-noon overhead to reflect equinoctial practice.

Global settings
- Aspect ratio: 16:9; Total: ~48 seconds.
- Style: vintage, semi-realistic; clean table; muted palette.
- Environment: simple courtyard/table; period bowl/cloth; avoid modern plastics/glossy paint.
- Camera: tripod-stable; exposure locked; fixed focus; macro ranges 15–40 cm; no zoom/rack focus/auto-reframe; hard cuts only; stable white balance.
- Light & wind: near-noon overhead light; short, straight shadow under objects; wind right→left (0.2–0.3 m/s); shadows minimal but coherent.
- Safety: no entrance/exit effects; no focus hunting; no exposure pulsing; avoid stabilization warps.
- Text & VO: IGNORE during generation (visuals-only). Timing for post.

Timeline & directions (0.0–48.0s)
0.0–12.0s (Segment A — Setup: eggs and surface)
- Visuals: place white plump eggs; inspect table grain; select flat patch; cloth wipe; show straight short shadow.
- Camera: close/macro; focus 20–30 cm; exposure locked; hard cuts.
- Physics: overhead light produces short shadow length (~1–2 cm); wind 0.2–0.3 m/s nudges cloth edge; shadow direction minimal.
- Details: avoid modern prints; keep surface matte.

12.0–24.0s (Segment B — Finger spin & balance attempt)
- Visuals: fingers spin egg gently; micro wobble; search for balance; contact patch tiny.
- Camera: macro on contact point; focus 15–25 cm; exposure locked; no rack focus.
- Physics: contact patch diameter ~3–5 mm; wobble amplitude ~2–4°; damping within ~2 s; wind minimal.
- Details: nails natural; avoid jewelry.

24.0–36.0s (Segment C — Balance achieved)
- Visuals: egg stands upright; micro corrections; second egg nearby fails then settles.
- Camera: close/macro; hold on balanced egg; shallow DOF; hard cuts.
- Physics: shadow short and straight; micro sway <1°; wind negligible.
- Details: table grain visible; cloth edge still.

36.0–48.0s (Segment D — Closing: stable hold)
- Visuals: hold on balanced egg(s); slight camera push-in (<3%); final clean frame.
- Camera: close hold; exposure/focus locked; no transition effects.
- Physics: shadow geometry consistent; wind minimal; end clean.
- Ending: clean stop.
