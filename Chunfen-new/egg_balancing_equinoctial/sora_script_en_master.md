# Sora Script (English) — Chunfen · Egg Balancing — ~48s, visuals-only

Objective (final policy): Master reference for a ~48-second visuals-only sequence (silent, no on-screen text). Generation will be executed as four clips (12s + 12s + 12s + 12s). No entrance/exit effects; transitions are direct cuts. Style: vintage aesthetic; physics: realistic light/shadow/wind. For this custom, light shifts to near-noon overhead to reflect equinoctial practice.

Global settings
- Aspect ratio: 16:9; Total: ~48 seconds.
- Style: vintage, semi-realistic; clean matte table; muted palette; subtle patina/wear; no glossy highlights.
- Environment: simple courtyard/table; period bowl/cloth; wood grain spacing ~2–4 mm; cloth fibers visible (~0.2–0.4 mm); avoid modern plastics/glossy paint.
- Camera: tripod-stable; exposure locked; fixed focus; macro ranges 15–40 cm; no zoom/rack focus/auto-reframe; hard cuts only; stable white balance; avoid digital sharpening halos.
- Light & wind: near-noon overhead light; short, straight shadow under objects (~1–2 cm length); wind right→left (0.2–0.3 m/s) nudges cloth edge only; shadows minimal but coherent; no heat shimmer.
- Safety: no entrance/exit effects; no focus hunting; no exposure pulsing; avoid stabilization warps; gentle hand motions.
- Text & VO: IGNORE during generation (visuals-only). Timing for post.
