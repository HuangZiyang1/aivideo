# Segment B — 15s — Knock and deliver “春牛图” print (Visuals-only)

Objective: Depict respectful knocking and the handover of a thin woodblock-printed “春牛图” paper, with tactile realism. Use Chinese characters exactly.

Global settings
- Style: vintage semi-realistic; dawn right-side light; warm highlights, soft shadows.
- Environment: weathered wooden gate; iron knocker; farmer just back from fields; no modern locks/varnish.
- Camera: direct hard cuts mid→close-up; tripod-stable; exposure/focus locked per shot; emphasize hands and paper fiber.
- Physics: wind right→left (0.3–0.4 m/s); paper edges lift 12–18°; during door opening, exterior light pushes through the gap (illumination change ΔEV≈0.7) tracing across the farmer’s cheekbone/brow ridge; fine dust drifts inward; shadows fall left consistently.
- Safety (visual): avoid glossy plastic sheen; no stabilization warps; no auto-exposure jumps between cuts.
 - Wardrobe/people (detailed): Spring Official’s dark-crimson satin robe with cloud-shoulder and “Spring Ox with herdboy” medallion readable; small-wing black gauze cap. Attendants (banner-bearer, small drummer) pass softly in background bokeh. Farmer wears coarse short jacket; mud marks on cuffs/knuckles; “dusty yet spirited.”
 - Text/Sora note: The paper must show the exact Chinese characters “春牛图” — do not translate or transliterate. Render as woodblock-printed Chinese glyphs with complete strokes and correct spacing.

Timeline (0.0–15.0s)
0.0–3.0s (mid): Spring Official knocks politely; show wrist/knuckle articulation; slight vibration of gate; micro dust release.
3.0–6.5s (close): From the inside latch moving, the door opens outward from one palm-width to two; warm daylight advances through the gap; the farmer leans into the gap — dusty face/jacket yet bright, resolute eyes. Action is latch-and-push (mud on knuckles, contact point with door visible), not purposeless finger flexing.
6.5–11.5s (close): Official presents the thin woodblock print — the top area clearly shows the three Chinese characters “春牛图” (woodblock style, even letter-spacing); paper fiber and deckled edge visible; edge lifts with breeze (period ~1.2 s).
11.5–15.0s (mid-close): Two-handed respectful handover; thumbs support lower edge; prevent paper buckle; shadows coherent; end clean on grip stabilization.
