# Segment B — 15s — Knock and deliver Spring Ox print (Visuals-only)

Objective: Depict respectful knocking and the handover of a thin woodblock-printed “Spring Ox” paper, with tactile realism.

Global settings
- Style: vintage semi-realistic; dawn right-side light; warm highlights, soft shadows.
- Environment: weathered wooden gate; iron knocker; farmer just back from fields; no modern locks/varnish.
- Camera: direct hard cuts mid→close-up; tripod-stable; exposure/focus locked per shot; emphasize hands and paper fiber.
- Physics: wind right→left (0.3–0.4 m/s); paper edges lift 12–18°; mud flakes shed with finger flexion; coherent shadow fall left.
- Safety (visual): avoid glossy plastic sheen; no stabilization warps; no auto-exposure jumps between cuts.

Timeline (0.0–15.0s)
0.0–3.0s (mid): Spring Official knocks politely; show wrist/knuckle articulation; slight vibration of gate; micro dust release.
3.0–6.5s (close): Cut to farmer’s hands; mud traces in finger webs; when fingers bend, 2–3 tiny flecks fall; maintain shallow DOF on hands.
6.5–11.5s (close): Official presents thin woodblock print (“Spring Ox”); show paper fiber, deckled edge; edges lift with breeze; angle oscillation period ~1.2s.
11.5–15.0s (mid-close): Two-handed respectful handover; thumbs support lower edge; prevent paper buckle; shadows coherent; end clean on grip stabilization.
