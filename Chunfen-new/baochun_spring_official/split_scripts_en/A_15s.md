# Segment A — 15s — Procession enters street (Visuals-only)

Objective: Open with a controlled, physics-consistent vintage procession led by the Spring Official. No text/audio. Hard cuts only.

Global settings
- Style: vintage, semi-realistic; early-spring dawn; muted earthy palette; gentle haze.
- Environment: narrow old street with courtyard gate; period-correct garments/props; avoid modern signage, plastics, glossy paint.
- Camera: chest-height medium-wide; tripod-stable; dolly forward ~3% max; exposure locked; focus distance fixed; no zoom, no auto-reframe.
- Physics: right-side sidelight; wind right→left (low→medium); shadows coherent to left; cloth/ribbons respond proportionally to wind.
- Safety (visual): no lens breathing; no focus hunting; no auto-exposure pulsing; no artificial entrance/exit effects.

Timeline (0.0–15.0s)
0.0–1.5s: Establish street and gate; hold stable framing; confirm exposure lock and left-falling shadow geometry.
1.5–4.0s: Spring Official (embroidered robe, hat) leads a small band into frame; banner staff enters from right, ribbon drag angle ~30–45° left.
4.0–7.0s: Subtle dolly forward (≤3%); staff tip taps ground once; micro dust rise; ribbons trail right→left with slight lag; avoid motion blur streaking.
7.0–11.5s: Villagers glance; sleeves flutter with wind amplitude A≈0.2–0.3 m/s; petal/confetti drift speed ~0.2 m/s left; keep camera inertial stability.
11.5–15.0s: Hold composition; damp any residual flutter; verify no exposure/focus shifts; end clean on frame; no transition.
