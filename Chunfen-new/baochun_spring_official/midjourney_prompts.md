# Chunfen · Baochun — MidJourney Keyframes

Guidelines
- Style: vintage aesthetic; semi-realistic textures; early-spring North China street/courtyard ambience.
- Physics consistency: right-side dawn light; wind right→left. Shadows coherent; flags/ribbons react naturally.
- No on-image text; focus on composition and mood. Use keyframes to calibrate Sora visuals style for each segment.
- Provide 1 opening keyframe per segment + 1 overall opening keyframe.
