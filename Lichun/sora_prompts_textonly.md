# Sora-Only Prompts — Lichun / Beginning of Spring (Textless)

Policy: Do NOT render any in-frame text. All titles, captions, and scripts (e.g., “Lìchūn”, custom names) are intended for post-production overlays only. Keep visuals stable and physically consistent.

Global settings:
- Aspect ratio: 16:9; Duration: 10 seconds per custom.
- Style: classical Chinese ink-wash over vintage yellow rice paper; semi-realistic textures.
- Light & wind: morning light from the right; wind flows right-to-left (low to medium); shadows respond to wind.
- Transitions: use ink-ripple reveal/retract when moving between rice paper and scene. If unstable, reduce to simple crossfade.

“16:9, duration 10s. Classical Chinese ink-wash over vintage yellow rice paper; semi-realistic. Morning light enters from the right; wind right-to-left, low to medium. No on-screen text.

3.3–6.6s (Main action): Ink-ripple crossfade (≈0.6s) to a street-level mid shot. The clay ox fills the mid frame; texture visible under right-side morning light. Wind-chime swings at 2–4° amplitude; one soft ring around 4.2s.

6.6–10.0s (Closure): Ink-ripple retract (≈0.6–0.8s) back to clean rice paper. Slight pull-back (55mm→50mm); hold stable to end. End on rice paper to bridge the next piece. Keep audio intent minimal (soft breeze, chime decay) if supported; otherwise silent.”
