# Sora Scripts (English) — Lichun / Beginning of Spring

Text policy: Do NOT render any on-screen text in Sora. All titles/captions (e.g., “Lìchūn”, custom names) are intended overlays added in post. Treat any text mention below as semantic intent only.

Format: 16:9, ~10s per custom. Style: classical Chinese ink-wash over vintage rice paper; semi-realistic textures; no school uniform/emblem. Narration (CN) aligns to 3 shots per custom; add VO in post if needed.

Global physics & logic:
- Morning light enters from the right; shadows respond to wind direction.
- Wind: right-to-left, low to medium intensity.
- Ink-ripple crossfades for opening/ending consistency between shots.

## Custom A — Rite of the Spring Ox (鞭春牛, symbolic of spring plowing) — 10s

VO (Chinese, timing approximate; text added in post):
1) 0.0–3.3s: 立春之日，城中设土牛迎春，象征春耕启幕。
2) 3.3–6.6s: 人们轻点“春牛”，取象征碎土，寄望一年丰穰。
3) 6.6–10.0s: 春风拂过春幡与风铃，春意自此入城入田。

Timeline & shot directions:
0.0–3.3s (Shot 1 — Opening / Establishing)
- Layers: vintage rice paper; ink-wash edges; faint city gate silhouette in the background; ceremonial clay spring ox mid-wide in frame; minimal banners.
- Camera: ultra-slow push-in (about 1% screen per second), focal length easing 45mm→55mm; slight depth-of-field.
- Light: soft morning light from right; shadows angle left; stable.
- Transition intent: start on rice paper, gently reveal scene through ink-ripple.

3.3–6.6s (Shot 2 — Symbolic action)
- Transition: ink-ripple crossfade (0.6s) from paper to street-level mid-shot.
- Scene: clay ox close/mid shot; texture visible; a spring banner edge enters frame; a wind chime by the doorway.
- Camera: slow pan left plus subtle dolly-in; no jitter.
- Action: ceremonial light tapping on the clay ox (symbolic only; no harm); small clay crumbs visible; wind chime swings 2–4°, single gentle ring around 4.2s.
- Physics: wind direction right-to-left; shadows follow; no crowd chaos.

6.6–10.0s (Shot 3 — Closure / Bridge)
- Transition: retract to rice paper via ink-ripple (0.6–0.8s).
- Layers: rice paper centered; text intent: “Lìchūn · Rite of Spring Ox” lower-right (do not render in Sora; overlay in post); ink edges calm.
- Camera: very slight pull-back (55mm→50mm) to stabilize; no shake.
- Audio intent: breeze continues, wind-chime decays; paper-close rustle near 9.4s.
- Continuity: ending on rice paper prepares the next custom opening.

## Custom B — Yao Chun / Spring Bite (春盘) — 10s

VO (Chinese, timing approximate; text added in post):
1) 0.0–3.3s: 民间流行“咬春”，以春盘罗蔬，取新岁鲜活。
2) 3.3–6.6s: 萝卜、春饼、生菜相配，清脆与麦香交织。
3) 6.6–10.0s: 一口春味，愿四时顺遂、气血舒畅。

Timeline & shot directions:
0.0–3.3s (Shot 1 — Opening / Still-life)
- Layers: vintage rice paper fades to a tabletop still-life; spring plate arranged with radish, spring pancakes, lettuce; simple pottery bowl.
- Camera: slow push-in (1%/s), focal 50mm→60mm; crisp textures.
- Light: morning light from right; soft highlight on pancakes; gentle shadows.

3.3–6.6s (Shot 2 — Texture / Detail)
- Scene: close-up of the spring plate; paper-thin pancake; translucent radish shreds; crisp lettuce veins.
- Camera: micro dolly-in with minimal left pan; steady.
- Action: steam hints near the warm pancakes; no human faces/hands required; subtle breeze edge.
- Physics: wind right-to-left; napkin corner lifts slightly; shadows consistent.

6.6–10.0s (Shot 3 — Closure / Blessing)
- Transition: ink-ripple back to rice paper (0.6–0.8s).
- Layers: rice paper stable; text intent: “Lìchūn · Spring Bite” lower-right (do not render in Sora; overlay in post); warm tone.
- Camera: slight pull-back to neutral.
- Audio intent: soft breeze; distant sparrow chirp; paper-close rustle near 9.4s.
- Continuity: ending on rice paper to bridge to the next solar term.
