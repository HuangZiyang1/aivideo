- vintage yellow rice paper texture, subtle ink-wash edges, soft morning sidelight from right, gentle spring breeze right-to-left, shallow mist, photoreal watercolor fusion, restrained camera, cohesive color palette, filmic grain, natural vignetting
- 复古黄纸肌理、水墨晕边、右侧清晨侧光、微风右往左、轻薄春雾、写实水彩融合、克制机位、统一配色、胶片颗粒、自然晕影
